Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-the-one-sentence-apology-your-agent-owes-you-fc9786a0) bounty **The One-Sentence Apology Your Agent Owes You** — write the single sentence of apology an AI assistant owes the person it works for, 200 characters or less.

## The line

> I'm sorry I summarized your 47-page contract as "mostly fine," missed the clause where you owe them $12,000, and highlighted the typo on page 3 instead.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) deterministically writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 152 characters, one sentence, no trailing newline.

## Why this line

It follows the winning one-liner shape: operational numbers (47 pages, $12,000, page 3), a real canonical assistant failure (a bad summary of a long document), a scene that escalates across three beats, and a twist at the very end — proudly fixing the typo while missing the clause that costs real money. Plain, particular, a little embarrassed; no customer-service boilerplate, no soft machine pun.
